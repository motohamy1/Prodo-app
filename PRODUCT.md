# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Personal productivity users and knowledge workers who manage tasks across work and personal life. Mobile-first: they capture, organize, and act on tasks throughout the day, often on the go. They move between quick captures and focused planning sessions. Context spans everything from grocery lists to sprint deadlines.

## Product Purpose

ToDoIt transforms scattered intentions into clear action. Success means the user trusts the app as their external brain: nothing falls through cracks, everything has its moment, and completing tasks feels genuinely rewarding rather than mechanical.

## Brand Personality

Bold, energetic, playful. The app has presence without shouting. It energizes the user to act rather than passively listing what they haven't done. The tone is supportive and direct — never corporate, never cutesy. Think confidence, not arrogance; playfulness, not childishness.

## Anti-references

- Cluttered enterprise tools (Jira, Asana density): too many fields, tables, dropdowns
- Generic SaaS aesthetic (white cards on gray, blue CTAs, stock illustrations)
- Gamified/toy-like apps (Habitica-style points, cartoon mascots, exaggerated animations)
- Brutalist/tech-bro aesthetic (monospace-only, dark-only, terminal nostalgia)

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity over density.** Every screen should feel breathable. If the user has to scan, the layout failed. Information hierarchy must be instant, not discovered.

2. **Momentum matters.** Interactions should create forward motion. Completion is a moment, not a checkbox. Transitions should feel like moving forward, not appearing and disappearing.

3. **Personality without gamification.** Delight through craft — typography, motion, color — not through points, streaks, or mascots. The interface itself is rewarding to use.

4. **Confidence through craft.** Every pixel intentional. No placeholder spacing, no default system components. The app should feel like it was designed, not assembled.

5. **Inclusive by design.** RTL is native, not an afterthought. Motion respects system preferences. Touch targets work for real fingers. The interface adapts to the user, not the other way around.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- RTL (Arabic) is a first-class language with full bidirectional layout support
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` — no animation that carries essential information
- Target WCAG AA contrast minimums for all text content
- Touch targets minimum 44x44pt on iOS, 48x48dp on Android
